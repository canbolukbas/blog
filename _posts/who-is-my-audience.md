(You can probably skip this post. I'm thinking out loud on this one)

Back in 2024, I was looking for magical pills. Pills for becoming a 10x engineer. I believe I found a good pill there: Federal Plain Language
Guidelines from US government. I plan to read it in the following days. I try to allocate 15 mins every day for becoming a better writer.

You can find the document here: https://www.plainlanguage.gov/media/FederalPLGuidelines.pdf

First chapter starts with the advice: Write for your audience. Makes sense! I should write for my audience whom is... Well. I don't know who is my audience. Well, whom could be? Why do I even write this blog? Is it for me? Is it for others?

Perhaps I don't have to determine it right now. My blog can have multiple audiences. I can specify whom I target on each post and what can they learn while reading this.
