The definition of meaningful life changes for most of us, while we don't really talk about it as much as I see from my experience. I'd like to share what I value in life for the last 2 months and I'm pretty excited (and a bit anxious) for sharing it.

I try to maximize the time spent for 'work' and 'lean' per day, and leave the rest. Think of it as an operating system, instead of a rational evaluation of navigating life.

An activity could be categorised as 'work' if it is connected to software engineering. My day job, reading a blog post, listening to a podcast, writing a blog post, practicing touch typing, testing LLMs, side projects...

An activity could be categorised as 'lean' if it is connected to an exercise. 'lean' means having a lean, muscular and healthy body. Walking, running, stretching, spinning, weight lifting...

I track them by hours. I try to aim for 8 hours of 'work'and 3 hours of 'lean', every day. I've vibe-coded a website for tracking it. During the day, I do whatever comes up to my mind in those categories, instead of deciding what is the best for me.

It has been a very effective system of mine. Obviously there are many details to it. Do let me know if you'd like to hear more about it.
