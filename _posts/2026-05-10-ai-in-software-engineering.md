---
title: "AI in Software Engineering"
date: 2026-05-10
---

AI leads to bloated codebases. Cost of producing new design, code and documentation is getting close to zero.

AI leads to increase in cognitive debt. Developers struggle to keep up with the changes in the codebase.

Engineers who can jump onto a codebase full of AI slop and untangle the mess will be the new elite. Perhaps a bare minimum.
