title: "This is it"
date: 2026-08-08
---

> What if this is it? Me waking up. Working for 8 hours under stress and ambiguity. Working out for an hour. Spending time with my family and going to sleep. Literally like every other person?

I wrote to my diary two days ago before going to sleep. The paragraph that is right above that is:

> I’ve stabilized my routine. I’m happy with it. It gives me a lot of comfort and joy. I can imagine myself doing this for many years. This scares me.

For some reason, I've always imagined this lifestyle ending at some point before 40. Me getting retired or me basically not working for anyone. This belief is conflicting with every day reality. What am I doing so special?

Meanwhile life might have surprises. I think I saw myself getting terminally ill in my dream last night. I woke up around 4am, and I wrote the below:

> I want to learn about interesting topics and dive deep. So that I can say if I understand I’m getting closer to my death, I have been doing some interesting things and enjoyed the process while doing the other necessary things to keep my stomach full and have a roof on top of my head without being a burden to my family
