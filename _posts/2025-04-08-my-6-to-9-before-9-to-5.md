title: "My 6-9 Before 9-5"
date: 2025-04-08
---

6 am. I wake up with alarm on my phone. There is only one alarm. To close the alarm, I need to get up from my bed. Once I close the alarm, I continue walking to the bathroom. I open all the lights in my apartment. I brush my teeth. I dress up the same clothes (all black) as every other day. Drink a good amount of water and prepare a coffee with my nespresso machine. Trying to be away from my phone while drinking my coffee.

6.30 am. I leave my apartment with my duffle gym bag. Not the most comfortable bag. I endure for now. I walk to the nearest metro station for 10mins. I hop onto the train and I hop off 2 stations later. I walk to my gym, Fitness First Haidhausen. I've been going there for 2 months and I like it very much. I arrive around 7am. I show my Wellpass membership and they give me the day entrance card.

7 am. I always start exercising with a stretching routine which takes ~10 mins. If it is my rest day, I usually cycle around ~20 mins. If not, I start lifting. I follow a training program called 5x5. It is a mixture of body building and powerlifting. Every training day starts with squats. Then it continues with bench press + dips/pull ups or deadlift + overhead press + bentover row. Each movement is 5 sets and 5 reps. I document my progress in a seperate Insta account of mine.

8 am. I shower at the gym. This a bit uncomfortable to me as there are no seperators at the showers. Everything is all over the place. However it feels great to have a shower after a tiring session. Good for the body.

8.30 am. I leave the gym. I buy a butter-breze from the bakery for 2€. I adore it. I walk to the metro station and take a train to the office.
